# Terms of use

Last updated: 12 September 2026. Questions: policy@devhelp.pk.

devhelp.pk is run by the devhelp team as a free, open-source project. By using it you agree to what is below. It is short on purpose.

## What you get

Everything on the platform is free. There are no fees, no paid tiers, no advertising, and nothing is upsold. You can read any lesson without an account; an account exists so the platform can keep your progress, award badges, and issue a certificate in your name.

We do not promise the platform will always be available, that it will be free of mistakes, or that finishing a course will get you a job. Content is provided as it is. What a certificate says is what you actually did — the lessons you completed and the criteria you met — and it is for an employer to weigh.

## What you may do with the content

- **The curriculum** — lessons, quizzes, exercises — is licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Use it, teach from it, translate it, print it for a class. Credit devhelp and share what you build from it under the same licence. A university or a bootcamp may run a whole course from it without asking us.
- **The platform's code** is licensed **MIT**, in the public repository. Run your own copy; it works on a laptop with Docker and no paid keys.
- **The brand** — the devhelp name, the mark, and the logo — is not covered by either licence. Do not present your own site or service as devhelp.

## What you contribute

- **Lessons, fixes and code** go through a pull request on one of the two public repositories, and you licence them under that repository's licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 for content, MIT for code.
- **Reviews, interview experiences and pay data** you licence to us to publish, indefinitely and without a fee, anonymised as described in the [privacy page](/privacy). You keep your own copyright.
- **You must have the standing to say it.** Write about your own experience: where you worked, what you were paid, the interview you sat. Do not post someone else's pay, someone else's review, or anything you agreed to keep confidential.
- **Everything a person submits is read by a moderator before publication**, against the [content policy](/policy). Rejections quote the clause. The rules that matter most: do not name individuals, do not make accusations you cannot stand behind, and do not post personal data.

We can hide or remove anything that breaks the content policy, and can suspend an account that does so repeatedly or that abuses other people. Where a decision was ours we will say which clause it was.

## Companies writing about themselves

A company representative can claim their company's profile, with a work-email domain match and an admin's approval, and reply publicly to what is written about them. A claim gives the right to reply. It does not give the right to edit, hide, or remove a review, and being a representative does not change the facts on the page. If something published about your company breaks the content policy, write to policy@devhelp.pk and it will be assessed against the policy like anything else.

## Using the platform fairly

Do not scrape the company bank, automate accounts, attempt to work around the contribution rules that gate it, or try to re-identify the author of an anonymous contribution. Do not attack the service or the people using it.

## Security research

If you find a vulnerability, tell us at policy@devhelp.pk before telling anyone else, and give us reasonable time to fix it. Do not access other people's data while testing. Reports are welcome and will be credited if you want.
