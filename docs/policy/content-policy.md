# Content policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026. Contact for takedown requests: policy@devhelp.pk.

devhelp.pk publishes reviews, interview experiences, salary information, questions, and answers written by people in Pakistan's software industry. Everything a person submits is read by a moderator before it is public, and anything public can be flagged by readers. Moderators apply the clauses below and quote the clause number when they reject or hide something.

## c1. Do not name individuals

Write about companies, teams, roles, and processes, not about named people. A manager's behaviour can be described; the manager cannot be named, described so precisely that colleagues would know who is meant, or linked to.

## c2. Only claim what you can stand behind

State what you saw and experienced. Accusations of crimes, fraud, harassment, or discrimination that you cannot substantiate are removed. "I was not paid for two months" is your experience; "the company steals from everyone" is not.

## c3. No personal data

Do not include phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses, ID numbers, photos of people, or private messages, yours or anyone else's.

## c4. Salaries are your own

Share only compensation you received or were offered yourself. Salaries reported "for a friend" or "for the whole team" are removed. Aggregates shown on the site are computed from at least five submissions.

## c5. One voice per person

One review and one salary point per company per person. Duplicate accounts, coordinated campaigns for or against a company, and reviews written by the company's own staff without disclosure are removed and the accounts closed.

## c6. Keep it on topic and readable

Reviews describe working at the company. Interview experiences describe the process. Questions on lessons are about the lesson. Advertising, recruitment pitches, unrelated links, and content that is mostly profanity are removed.

## c7. New accounts and links

Links posted by accounts younger than seven days are held for review before they appear. This limits spam without stopping anyone from participating.

## c8. Companies can ask for a review

A company can ask a moderator to look at a specific post through the "request review" form. The request, the moderator's decision, and the reason are logged and visible to administrators. Posts are not removed on request alone; they are removed when they break a clause above.

## c9. Mentors

Mentor applications are decided by administrators. Mentors moderate only the tracks they were granted, and every moderation action they take is logged with their name and reason.

## Takedown requests

If you believe content about you or your company breaks this policy, email policy@devhelp.pk with the link and the clause. We reply within five working days. Legal notices go to the same address.
